## Findings

### 1. Keep-alive is the robust, dominant win — at every scale
greedy → greedy+keep-alive cuts cold starts by **79% (100u)** down to **~19% (5000u)**
and turnaround 53%→3%. warm_rate, energy all improve. CIs do not overlap greedy.
This is the solid, defensible ablation result.

### 2. ⚠️ The PREDICTIVE (prewarm) variant DEGRADES at scale — this is the headline issue
predictive is **best at low load** (100u: −80% cold, marginally beating keep-alive)
but **gets progressively worse as load grows**, and at the flagship **5000-user
scale it is WORSE THAN PLAIN GREEDY** (cold starts +21.6%, turnaround +4.3%, lowest
warm_rate 0.899, worst p99 1565 ms). Crossover vs keep-alive happens between 500u
and 1000u.

This must be understood **before** the predictive method goes into the paper as the
proposal — right now the data says "our proposed method is worst at the largest
scale." Likely cause: **prewarm thrashes the small warm pool.** `MAX_WARM_PER_NODE=16`;
prewarm proactively `admit_cold`s predicted functions, which LRU-evict actually-warm
ones. At high user counts many users prewarm onto the same hot nodes, so prewarm
evicts more warmth than it creates. The prewarm tail also shows up as a higher p99
(1565–1569 ms) at 1000u/5000u (migration/switch cost).

**Action items before using predictive:**
- Sensitivity sweep on `MAX_WARM_PER_NODE` (e.g. 16 → 64 → 256) at 1000u/5000u — does
  a larger pool restore predictive's advantage? If yes, it was a capacity artifact;
  pick a defensible pool size and document it.
- Check prewarm admission vs eviction counts (`pool_evictions`, `rejected_count`
  columns) at 5000u to confirm the thrash hypothesis.
- Consider gating prewarm by spare pool capacity (don't prewarm if it would evict a
  live container).

### 3. Among the three warm variants, differences are within CI at low load
At 100u, keep-alive / pred-noWS / predictive cold starts (4944 / 4894 / 4740) overlap
heavily (±~2700). Do not claim a significant predictive>keep-alive edge at small
scale. The meaningful, significant contrast everywhere is greedy vs (any warm variant).

### 4. Benefit compresses with load
cold-start reduction of keep-alive vs greedy: 79% (100u) → ~19% (500–5000u). As the
10 edges + 32-function pool saturate, the reuse headroom (and thus every variant's
edge over greedy) shrinks.
